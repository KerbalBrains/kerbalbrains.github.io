Build a colony that feeds itself on another world

Drop a Command Center, inflate greenhouses, dig ore and smelt it into Metal so the colony can build its own buildings. Skip the launch with Colony Express, drive the colony rover, and with Kerbal Brains installed, hand every Kerbal a job with one button.

v0.7.2 KSP 1.12 Stock and JNSQ Sandbox, Science, Career Best with Kerbal Brains

New since the first test: mining and Metal, Colony Express, the colony rover and garage, the job board, a 3D Command Center, and a loading screen soundtrack.

Getting there

Two ways to start a colony

FAST · from the Space Center

Colony Express

  1. At the Space Center, click the IronRoot button on the toolbar.
  2. Pick a world, a flat landing spot and up to 3 Kerbals.
  3. Press Land! You arrive in flight next to a Command Center kit, a rover and starter Metal.
CLASSIC · fly it yourself

Land your own ship

  1. Land any ship. Flat ground is easiest.
  2. In flight, click the IronRoot toolbar button (glass dome with a green sprout).
  3. Click Found a colony here and drop the green ghost Command Center.

Materials

From dirt to new buildings

Every building except the first Command Center costs Metal. The colony pays from its own Metal first, then from RocketParts on landed ships within 400 m.

Mine

2 Miners dig 4 Ore an hour. EVA Kerbals in the pit add more.

Smelt

4 Ore becomes 2 Metal an hour. Needs 12 power.

Store

The Command Center holds 200 Metal, each Smelter 300 more.

Build

A Greenhouse is 150 Metal, about 75 hours of one Smelter.

Testing? Tick Free building (no materials needed) in the IronRoot window to skip costs.

Colony Express

Skip the launch

Open it from the IronRoot button at the Space Center. The Super-Heavy Lander drops you on a flat spot with everything a new colony needs.

How it works

  1. Pick a world. Any planet or moon with ground.
  2. Pick a landing spot. The list shows the flattest spots it can find, plus "next to my colony" spots.
  3. Pick up to 3 Kerbals. Only Kerbals who are free at the Astronaut Complex.
  4. Press Land! You start in flight with the lander, a Command Center kit, the rover and starter Metal. A puff of dust shows where you touched down.

Game modes

SandboxFree and instant. Any world.
ScienceFree, but only worlds you've reached (flyby, orbit or landing).
CareerOnly worlds you've reached, and the trip costs funds.
45,000 funds Farther from Kerbin's orbit costs more; moons of other planets add 20,000. Uses the same formula as the mod, with stock-style distances. The window in the game shows the exact price.

    Crew and rover

    Getting Kerbals to work

    Built in

    The colony rover

    Every Command Center comes with a garage and a free rover. It rolls out on its own once the Command Center is built.

    Call the rover brings it back to the garage. Roll out the rover makes a new one if yours is lost.

    Needs Kerbal Brains

    The job board

    Press Put everyone to work. Builders go to unfinished buildings, miners to the pit, plus a farmer, a smelter worker and someone at the Command Center. Everyone else explores.

    Far jobs? Kerbals hop in the colony rover and drive there.

    How it works

    The colony loop

    Your colony keeps running when you fly away, switch ships or time warp. It catches up the moment you come back.

    Staff

    Colonists fill jobs: greenhouses first, then solar, mines and smelters. Spare colonists show as idle.

    Power

    Solar and the Command Center make power. If buildings use more, the battery covers the gap until it runs dry.

    Produce

    Staffed, powered buildings make food, oxygen, Ore and Metal. Half-staffed means half the output.

    Survive

    Every colonist eats and breathes each hour. Storage has a limit, so extra is wasted.

    Grow

    48 good hours in a row bring a new colonist. Good means fed, breathing, powered and a spare bed.

    Out of oxygenColonists shelter inside and all work drops to 25%.
    Out of foodGrowth stops and work drops to 50%.
    Low powerMachines slow down to match the power you have.

    No Kerbals die. A hardcore mode may come later as an option.

    Troubleshooting

    Something not working?

    I don't see the IronRoot toolbar button

    Restart KSP. In flight it's the glass dome with a green sprout. At the Space Center the same button opens Colony Express. Check that GameData/IronRoot/Plugins/IronRoot.dll exists.

    Colony Express says a world is "not reached yet"

    In Science and Career you need a flyby, orbit or landing there first. Sandbox lets you go anywhere.

    The ghost building is red

    The message at the top of the screen says why: too far from the Command Center (400 m) or your ship (2.5 km), overlapping another building, not enough Metal, or you're in map view or time warp.

    "Needs Metal" or "RocketParts isn't installed"

    Build a Strip Mine and a Smelter and let them run, park a ship with RocketParts within 400 m, or tick Free building.

    The rover is gone or far away

    Press Call the rover in the IronRoot window. If it was destroyed, the button says Roll out the rover and makes a new one.

    "The job board needs the Kerbal Brains mod"

    The Put everyone to work button only works when Kerbal Brains is installed too.

    My buildings disappeared

    Buildings only show within 25 km of the ship you're flying. The colony still runs. Fly back and they reappear.

    No music while loading

    Check that the four .ogg files are in GameData/IronRoot/PluginData/Music and that the Music volume in Settings isn't at zero.

    I want to change the numbers

    Open GameData/IronRoot/Buildings.cfg in Notepad, change any rate or cost, and restart KSP. No coding needed.

    IRONROOT_BUILDING
{
	name = IronRoot_Smelter
	cost = 180
	title = Smelter
	RATES
	{
		Ore = -4
		Metal = 2      // try 4 for a super smelter
		Power = -12
	}
}
    Something broke or looks weird

    Write down what you did and which planet you were on, and take a screenshot. Every bug report makes the next version better.
