Step 1

Install

  1. Back up your saves first. Copy the saves folder inside your KSP folder somewhere safe. It takes ten seconds and protects your games.
  2. Close KSP. Unzip the download. Inside is a GameData folder with KerbalJumpGate in it.
  3. Copy KerbalJumpGate into Kerbal Space Program/GameData. On Steam that is usually Steam/steamapps/common/Kerbal Space Program.
  4. Start KSP. You will see Jump Gate pictures and tips on the loading screen, like “Loading Kerbal Jump Gate...”. That means it worked.

Works with stock planets and with planet packs like JNSQ. Gates place themselves at a safe height above any planet, so you never have to set numbers.

Step 2

Your first jump: Kerbin to Duna

Every game starts with two gates already in orbit: the Kerbin Gate and the Duna Gate. They are always on, glowing and pulsing, waiting for you.

  1. Launch any ship and get into orbit around Kerbin.
  2. Open the Jump Gate window and go to the Gates tab. Next to Kerbin, press Target. You can also click the gate's ring marker on screen or in map view.
  3. Now it works like any target: navball markers, and closest approach in map view. Plan a meeting with it like you would with a space station. MechJeb's rendezvous autopilot can fly it for you too.
  4. Or press Fly to:
    • Sandbox: it hops your ship straight to the start of the lane, lined up and rolling in.
    • Career: get within 5 km first. Then the autopilot does the last part with real engine burns. Press Stop to take over any time.
  5. Fly through the swirl. Hold on. You come out at Duna, and the Duna Gate is already set to take you home.
Step 3

The rainbow lane and the capture beam

Seven glowing rings float out from each side of every gate, like a runway in space. Red is the farthest out and violet is closest to the gate. They pulse inward toward the swirl, and the pulse speeds up as you get closer.

Line up with the bullseye

Within about 2.5 km, a target appears on your screen. The dot shows where your ship is compared with the center of the gate. Get it in the middle and point your nose at the gate.

Kerbin Gate >> Duna

  • Green means lined up. Yellow is close. Red means you would miss.
  • Your ship has to pass through the middle of the swirl to jump. Clipping the edge does nothing.

The capture beam

Head toward the gate with your nose roughly pointed at it, inside 2 km. The beam locks on, turns off SAS, straightens your ship and reels you in to the center. Hands off while it works. It gives SAS back when you arrive.

The beam lets go if you turn away, drift too far off to the side, or fly past. Back up and come in again.

The jump and the arrival

When you hit the swirl, the movie camera cuts to a side shot, the tunnel spins, and you pop out of the gate at the other planet. Press any key to skip the camera shot.

You arrive glowing in that planet's color, with energy trails peeling off your ship, while the gate breathes out a wave of light: orange at Duna, purple at Eve, teal at Laythe, icy white at Eeloo.

The gate you arrive at is already set to send you back the way you came.

Step 4

  • Locked planets have no gate yet. The dial tells you what is needed.

Step 5

Build your own gate

The Kerbal Jump Gate (Folded) is a 3.75 m, 12 ton part. Fly it to a planet, deploy it in orbit, and that planet joins the network.

  1. In the VAB, open the Utility parts or search jump gate.
  2. Right-click the part and choose Preview unfolded to see a see-through, full-size 40 m gate around your rocket, swirl spinning. Click again to fold it away.
  3. Put the gate on the very top (or very bottom) of the stack. It can't deploy from the middle.
  4. Fly to the planet and get into a stable orbit, above the air and the mountains. Stop time warp.
  5. Right-click the gate and choose Deploy Jump Gate. If it says no, it tells you why.

Then watch the show

1 · Drift clear

The gate floats 300 m away from your ship on its little thrusters, so you can see the whole thing.

2 · Unfold and power up

It unfolds into a 40 m ring. Then comes 15 seconds of rumble, racing lights, crackling arcs, a boom and a shockwave as the swirl ignites.

3 · Always on

The rainbow rings shoot out one by one. The gate stays on from then on, and Kerbin's gate now points to it.

The movie camera follows the whole deploy. Press any key to skip straight to the finished gate.

Career mode

Unlocking the network

In career and science games, gates are something you earn.

  • Research the tech. The folded gate is a late part in the tech tree. The window shows which node you need.
  • Orbit a planet before you can gate it. Reaching orbit there unlocks deploying a gate around it.
  • Link planets in order. Each new planet needs the one before it.
  • Contracts. Mission Control offers a contract for each next gate. You also get a bonus if you land on that planet after accepting.
  • Moons can be gated any time once you have orbited them.

The planet order

Kerbin+Duna→Eve→Moho→Dres→Jool→Laythe→Tylo→Eeloo

Kerbin and Duna start linked, so your first contract is a gate at Eve.

Know your gate

What the gate does

It breathes

Every 8 to 15 seconds a funnel of swirl reaches out of one side, spins, and gets sucked back in with a whoosh.

Its lights chase

The seven diamond lights chase round the ring, slowly cycling through every color of the rainbow.

It has a heartbeat

The swirl and ring flare on every beat, in time with the rainbow rings. The closer you get, the faster it beats.

Stuck?

Help

I flew through and nothing happened

Go through the middle of the swirl, not the edge. Check the gate isn't still powering up (the dial shows a countdown). There must be at least one other gate to go to.

The capture beam won't grab me

Be inside 2 km, moving toward the gate, with your nose roughly pointed at it. If it let go, fly back out a few hundred meters and come in again.

Deploy Jump Gate says no

Read the message. Common ones: stop time warp, put the gate at the end of the stack, raise your orbit, or (career) research the tech, orbit the planet, or link the planet before it first.

I closed the dial and it won't come back

Open the Jump Gate window, Gates tab, and press Dial next to the gate.

Where is the gate part in the VAB?

In Utility, or search jump gate. In career it appears once you research its tech node.

The game feels slow near a gate

Set quality = Low in Settings.cfg, or turn off the approach rings in the Settings tab.

Sandbox: what is the Test bench?

Sandbox games get a Test bench tab with shortcuts: go to orbit around any planet, spawn and deploy a folded gate, jump next to any gate, and try career rules without a career save.
