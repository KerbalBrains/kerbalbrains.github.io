Kerbal Brains

Your Kerbals get a mini brain. They climb out, fix stuff, plant flags, mine, chat, dance, eat lunch, ride the crew bus and fly jetpacks, all on their own. Or give them orders and jobs.

Controls

Buttons and keys

Pink brain button (flight)The crew window: brains on/off, Everyone OUT / IN with an "X of Y out" count, voices, volume, Lunch time, Antennas, Cinematic camera, Give a job.
Right-click a Kerbal on EVABrain orders: Follow me, Go here, Drive the rover here, Explore, Guard, Victory dance, Group photo, Plant a flag, Take a nap, Give a job, Back to normal.
Brain button (Space Center, VAB, SPH)Crew on/off for each building, Lunch truck, Brain antennas, Trucks, bus and crawler, Cinematic camera.
Ctrl + KStart or stop the cinematic camera. Click or press Esc to stop it too.
IronRoot colony window"Put everyone to work" sends builders, miners, a farmer and a smelter worker to their jobs. Everyone else explores.
Space Center toolbarIronRoot Colony Express: pick a world, a flat spot and a crew, then land without launching.
