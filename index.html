Getting started

Install in three steps

Made for KSP 1.12.x on a Windows PC. The launcher does the heavy lifting, and every mod still has its own guide inside its bay.

The Chef's recipe. Three steps and your crew is ready to cook. Lunch is on us.

Back up your save

Copy your KSP saves folder somewhere safe before adding any mod.

Get the launcher

Download the free Kerbal Brains Launcher and run setup. It finds your KSP folder and installs itself.

DOWNLOAD

Install and launch

Open the Mods Hub, click Install on any mod, then press Launch KSP. Free mods need no sign-in.

Play from Steam: in Steam, right-click KSP, open Properties, and set Launch Options to "...\LightspeedLauncher.exe" --steam %command% (use the real path to your KSP folder). Now the Play button opens the launcher first.

Prefer doing it by hand? Every mod also comes as a zip. Install ModuleManager and Harmony from CKAN or SpaceDock, then unzip the mod into GameData.
